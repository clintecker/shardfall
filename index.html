A turn-based conquest for one commander

Shardfall

Four future cultures share one broken slab of world. Lead one of them: scout the dark, take outposts, grow your cities, research new hardware, and hold the most ground when turn 30 ends, or take every rival city before then.

Each city supports a limited army, so growing cities matters as much as taking them. Tap a unit to see where it can move (glowing tiles) and what it can hit (red tiles). A unit that starts its turn on someone else's city can capture it. Tap your city to train units; tap a resource inside your borders to harvest it and grow the city.
